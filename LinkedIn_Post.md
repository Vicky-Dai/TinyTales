# LinkedIn Post for Team 17 - 3rd Place

Excited to share that our team secured 3rd place at the Seattle Data AI Security Hackathon! Last week was an incredible experience participating in the hackathon hosted by Seattle Data AI & Security at the City University of Seattle. It was a day filled with coding, innovation, and intense problem-solving. My team and I developed StorySprout AI, an intelligent web application designed to revolutionize how children's stories are created and shared. 

💡 What we built:
We created an AI-powered platform that transforms simple story ideas into complete illustrated children's books. Key features include:

📚 Smart Story Generation: Converting user inputs (characters, setting, moral lessons) into 5-6 page engaging stories with age-appropriate vocabulary

🎨 AI-Generated Illustrations: Creating beautiful, kid-safe illustrations for each page of the story

🔊 Text-to-Speech Narration: Optional child-friendly voice narration to bring stories to life

🛡️ Built-in Safety & Content Moderation: Ensuring all content is appropriate for young readers through OpenAI Moderation API and strict safety filters

It was a challenge to build this full-stack solution with React, FastAPI, and multiple AI services under strict time constraints (5 hours!), but I'm incredibly proud of the result!

And a massive shoutout to my amazing teammates — we pushed through the challenges together and delivered a product that truly makes a difference for parents, teachers, and caregivers!
